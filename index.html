Air traffic control games for Android: the main types and what to play

Android ATC games come in three kinds: arcade route drawing, radar approach sims and airport tycoons. Here is how they differ and which games exist.

Double elimination bracket explained

In double elimination a team is out after two losses. N teams play 2N-2 games, or 2N-1 if the grand final is reset. How the losers bracket works.

How many games are in a tournament?

Single elimination: N-1 games. Double elimination: 2N-2 or 2N-1. Round robin: N(N-1)/2. Swiss: rounds × N/2. Table for 4 to 32 teams.

How to keep livestock health records

Log each animal's ID and every treatment: product, batch, dose, date, who gave it and withdrawal end date. UK and EU rules: keep 5 years or more.

How to learn Morse code by ear

Learn Morse by sound, not a chart: hear characters at 15 to 20 WPM, start with two (Koch method), add one at 90% accuracy, practice daily.

How to organize a round robin tournament

In a round robin of N teams everyone plays everyone: N(N-1)/2 games in N-1 rounds (N rounds if odd). How to schedule, handle byes and break ties.

How to seed a tournament bracket

Rank the teams, then pair 1 vs 16, 8 vs 9, 4 vs 13 and so on, so top seeds meet last. Byes = next power of two minus teams, given to top seeds.

Satisfying cleaning games without timers or pressure

Calm cleaning and restoration games have no timers, lives or energy. What makes them satisfying, how to spot pressure, and games to try on Android.

How to use a soccer tactics board to explain plays

Show one idea per board, move it step by step, keep the same arrows, then practice it on the field. Formations, pressing, set pieces, a sample plan.

Swiss system tournament explained

In a Swiss tournament players with equal scores meet each round, nobody plays twice and nobody is eliminated. About log2 N rounds, Buchholz breaks ties.

How to calculate voltage drop

Single-phase VD = 2 × K × I × L ÷ CM; three-phase uses 1.732. K is 12.9 for copper, 21.2 for aluminum. Worked examples in AWG and mm².
