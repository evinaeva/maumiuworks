Features

No signal on site, but you still need wire size, voltage drop and a labelled panel? Wirekit works offline, with no ads and no sign-up.

Wirekit is an electrician app that works without internet: wire size and voltage drop calculators, a breaker panel designer with a printable circuit directory, a wiring trainer that checks your circuits, and a quick electrical reference. No sign-up: open it and start working.

ELECTRICAL CALCULATORS

  • Wire size by current (ampacity): NEC 310.16 in AWG/kcmil with 60/75/90 °C columns, ambient temperature, number of current-carrying conductors in a conduit and the small-conductor rule, or IEC 60364-5-52 in mm² with installation methods A1/B1/C, PVC or XLPE, copper or aluminium, temperature and grouping factors.
  • Voltage drop for single-phase and three-phase circuits, with a clear 3 % / 5 % check.
  • Ohm's law and power: enter any two of volts, amps, ohms and watts, plus power factor.
  • Motor current from kW or hp, single- or three-phase, with efficiency and power factor.
  • Bending conduit: offsets, 3-point saddles and 90° stub-ups with deduct, in inches or centimetres.
  • AWG ↔ mm² converter.

BREAKER PANEL DESIGNER & CIRCUIT DIRECTORY

Build each panel breaker by breaker: MCB, RCBO, RCD or MCCB, rating, B/C/D curve, poles, RCD sensitivity in mA, wire size and the room or label. See the breaker box as a DIN-rail view, then export the panel schedule and labels as a PDF and share it with your customer. Back up and restore all panels to a file: your data stays on your phone.

WIRING TRAINER

Practise verified circuits: a one-way switch and lamp, 3-way (two-way) staircase switching, a receptacle on an RCD, lamps in parallel vs series, and a contactor start/stop circuit with a self-holding (latching) contact, plus an open sandbox. Tap one terminal, then another, and the wire snaps into place. Pick wire colours, undo, zoom and pan; your work is saved automatically.
Press "Test circuit" and the trainer tries every switch position and shows what is wrong: a switch in the neutral, a short circuit that trips the breaker, the RCD test button, a missing ground (PE). Export the wiring diagram as an image.

ELECTRICAL REFERENCE

Wire colours by standard (IEC 60445 / EU, US NEC, old UK, Brazil NBR 5410, Russia), breaker trip curves B/C/D, IP ratings, an AWG ↔ mm² table and typical home circuits.

WHY WIREKIT

  • Your data stays on your phone: everything is saved on the device, and panels can be backed up to a file.
  • Works offline: no login, nothing to sync, no personal data.
  • Your standard and units: NEC or IEC, imperial or metric.
  • Easy to read on the job: light and dark theme, high contrast and larger text.

Important: calculations follow the NEC 310.16 and IEC 60364-5-52 tables, and the wiring trainer is a simplified teaching model. Always check results against your local code and have electrical work done or inspected by a qualified electrician.

Questions and answers

How do I size wire and a breaker for a circuit?

Open Wire size, keep NEC selected and enter the load current. Wirekit uses NEC Table 310.16 (2023) in AWG/kcmil, copper or aluminum, with the 60/75/90 °C columns, ambient temperature, the number of current-carrying conductors and the 125 % rule for continuous loads. It shows the minimum wire size, the next standard breaker or fuse size and the 240.4(D) small-conductor limit. Check the result against your local code and have a licensed electrician do or inspect the work.

Is there a conduit fill calculator that works offline?

Yes. Box and conduit fill works with no signal at all. Pick EMT, PVC Schedule 40 or RMC, a trade size from 1/2 to 3 inches and THHN/THWN-2 wire from 14 AWG to 4/0. Wirekit shows the fill against the NEC Chapter 9 limits (53 %, 31 % or 40 %), how many conductors of that size fit and the smallest conduit that works. The same screen does NEC 314.16 box fill for standard metal device, octagon and square boxes.

Does Wirekit have a conduit bending calculator?

Yes. Conduit bending gives the marks and step-by-step instructions for offsets, rolling offsets, kicks, 3-point and 4-point saddles, 90° stub-ups, back-to-back 90s and segmented bends. Enter the offset depth or obstacle height and the bend angle, and it shows the multiplier, the shrink and where to mark the pipe, in inches rounded to 1/16 or in centimeters. For stub-ups you enter your own bender's take-up; typical EMT values are listed as a starting point.

How do I calculate voltage drop on a long run?

Open Voltage drop, choose DC, single-phase or three-phase and enter the voltage, current, one-way length and conductor size. Wirekit shows the drop in volts and percent, the voltage at the load and the smallest wire that stays within your limit: 3 % for a branch circuit or 5 % for feeder plus branch. In NEC mode it uses Chapter 9 Table 8 resistance and ignores reactance, so long runs with large conductors read slightly low.

Can I make a printable panel schedule?

Yes. In Panels you build a breaker box space by space: breaker type, amp rating, poles, what each circuit feeds, wire size and notes. Start empty or from a house template, view it as a plan or a panel drawing, then export a PDF with the circuit directory for the panel door and labels to cut out and stick next to each breaker. You can also share the schedule as text and back up all panels to a file.

Does Wirekit work without internet?

Yes. All calculators, including the NEC 220 house load and service size, plus the panel designer, the wiring trainer and the reference work in airplane mode, with no account and no ads. Your panels are saved on the phone and leave it only when you export or share a file or publish a panel's QR page yourself. When online, the app sends anonymous daily usage counts, with no device ID and nothing you type; you can turn this off in Settings.
