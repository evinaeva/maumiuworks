Tournament bracket generator

Enter teams or players in seed order and get a single or double elimination bracket with proper seeding: 1 meets the lowest seed, and byes go to the top seeds. Tap a name to send it to the next round. No sign-up, it works in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Format

The first line is seed 1. 2 to 128 names (3 or more for double elimination).

Your bracket

Tap or click a name (or Tab to it and press Enter) to make it the winner of that match; the winner moves on by itself. Tap the winner again to clear the result. If you change a result, later results that no longer fit are cleared, and Undo brings them back.

How the bracket is built

Seeding

The list order is the seeding: the first name is seed 1. The bracket uses the standard seeding order, so the strongest seeds can only meet late: in an 8-team bracket the first round is 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 2 v 7, 3 v 6, seeds 1 and 2 are in opposite halves and can meet only in the final, and seeds 1 to 4 are in different quarters. In a 16-team bracket the first round is 1 v 16, 8 v 9, 4 v 13, 5 v 12, 2 v 15, 7 v 10, 3 v 14, 6 v 11. "Random draw" shuffles the list if you want a draw instead of a seeding.

Byes

A bracket needs a power of two (2, 4, 8, 16, 32…) places. If you have fewer teams, the bracket is filled up to the next power of two and the empty places are byes. The byes go to the highest seeds, who move straight to round 2.

Byes = next power of two − N

Example: 12 teams use a 16-place bracket, so there are 16 − 12 = 4 byes for seeds 1 to 4, and 4 first-round matches between seeds 5 to 12.

Single elimination

Lose once and you are out. Every match knocks out one team and all but the champion must be knocked out, so there are N − 1 games in ⌈log₂ N⌉ rounds: 16 teams play 15 games in 4 rounds. A third-place match adds one game.

Double elimination

A team is out only after its second loss. Everyone starts in the winners bracket; a first loss drops a team into the losers bracket, where it keeps playing until it loses again. Teams dropping down from the winners bracket are placed so that two teams who have just played each other do not meet again straight away (only with 3 teams is that impossible to avoid). The winners-bracket champion meets the losers-bracket champion in the grand final.

  • If the winners-bracket champion wins the grand final, the tournament is over: 2N − 2 games.
  • If the losers-bracket champion wins, both teams now have one loss. With grand final reset on, a second, deciding final is played: 2N − 1 games.

Example: 8 teams play 14 games, or 15 when the reset is needed.

Questions and answers

How are teams seeded in a bracket?

The order of your list is the seeding, and the bracket uses the standard order so top seeds meet as late as possible. With 8 teams the first round is 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 2 v 7 and 3 v 6, so seeds 1 and 2 can only meet in the final. Use Random draw to shuffle the list if you want a draw instead.

How many byes does a bracket need?

A bracket has a power-of-two number of places: 4, 8, 16, 32 and so on. Byes = next power of two − number of teams. 12 teams use a 16-place bracket with 4 byes, 20 teams a 32-place bracket with 12 byes. The byes go to the highest seeds, who start in round 2.

How many games are in a single elimination tournament?

Always one less than the number of teams, N − 1, because every game knocks out exactly one team and all but the champion are knocked out. 16 teams play 15 games in 4 rounds. A third-place match adds one game.

How many games are in a double elimination tournament?

2N − 2 games if the winners-bracket champion wins the grand final, and 2N − 1 if the losers-bracket champion wins it and a reset final is played. 8 teams play 14 or 15 games, 16 teams 30 or 31.

What is a grand final reset?

In double elimination the winners-bracket champion reaches the grand final unbeaten, while the losers-bracket champion already has one loss. If the losers-bracket champion wins the grand final, both have one loss, so a second final decides the title. You can switch the reset off if you only have time for one final.

Can I share or save the bracket?

Yes. Copy link puts the names, options and every result into the link itself, so whoever opens it sees the same bracket. You can also copy the bracket as text, download a CSV file or print it. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
