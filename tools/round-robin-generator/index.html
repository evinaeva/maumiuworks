    How the round robin schedule is made

    The schedule uses the circle method (also called the Berger method). Write the teams in two rows facing each other: the top row plays the bottom row. The first team stays in place and everyone else moves one seat around the circle each round, so after the last round every pair has met exactly once.

    Games = N × (N − 1) ÷ 2

    • Even number of teams: N − 1 rounds, N ÷ 2 games in every round, nobody rests.
    • Odd number of teams: an empty seat (a bye) is added, so there are N rounds and one team sits out each round. Every team rests exactly once.
    • Home and away: the whole schedule is played a second time with home and away swapped, so games and rounds double. With an even number of teams the second half starts from round 2 of the first half, so nobody meets the same opponent two rounds running.

    Example: 6 teams

    6 × 5 ÷ 2 = 15 games in 5 rounds of 3 games. With 7 teams it is 7 × 6 ÷ 2 = 21 games in 7 rounds of 3 games, and one team has a bye each round. With home and away, 6 teams play 30 games in 10 rounds.

    Home and away inside a single round robin are spread so that a team does not play many home or many away games in a row. The first name in each game is the home team. "Shuffle order" changes who meets whom in which round; the number of games stays the same.

    Questions and answers

    How many games are in a round robin?

    A round robin of N teams has N × (N − 1) ÷ 2 games. 4 teams play 6 games, 6 teams play 15, 8 teams play 28 and 10 teams play 45. With home and away (a double round robin) every pair meets twice, so the number doubles: 8 teams play 56 games.

    How many rounds does a round robin take?

    With an even number of teams it takes N − 1 rounds and nobody rests: 8 teams need 7 rounds of 4 games. With an odd number it takes N rounds and one team has a bye in each round: 7 teams need 7 rounds of 3 games. Home and away doubles the rounds.

    What happens with an odd number of teams?

    An empty seat is added to the circle. Whoever is drawn against it has a bye and sits out that round. Every team gets exactly one bye in a single round robin, so everyone plays the same number of games (N − 1).

    What is the circle method?

    It is the standard way to make a round robin schedule, also called the Berger method. One team stays fixed and the others rotate one place each round, so every pair meets exactly once and no team plays twice in the same round.

    Does shuffling change the number of games?

    No. Shuffle order only changes which teams meet in which round. The number of games and rounds depends only on how many teams there are and on whether you play home and away.

    Can I keep or share the schedule?

    Yes. Copy it as text for a chat, download it as a CSV file for a spreadsheet, print it, or copy the link: the names and options are stored in the link itself, so opening it shows the same schedule again. Nothing is uploaded.
