Oddly Satisfying Restoration — Privacy Policy

No account, no ads, no advertising ID. The game sends us anonymous gameplay statistics, which you can switch off in Settings.

  • Your progress stays on your phone. Cleaned items, story progress and settings are stored in the game's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Google Play Games cloud save. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game also saves the same progress to your Play Games account, so it survives a new phone. Google stores it under Google's terms; we cannot see it.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics. To learn which parts of the game people enjoy and where they stop, the game sends short events to our own server: which mode you open (story or free cleaning), which scene you enter and leave and how long you stay, which cleaning step you reach, whether you watch a cut-scene or skip it (and at which second), when a session starts and when the game goes to the background, plus the game version, game language and Android version.
  • A random number, not you. Events carry a random installation number that the game creates on first launch. It is not your advertising ID or a device ID and is not linked to your name, Google account or email. We do not store IP addresses. No third-party analytics service is used; the data is never sold or shared. Events are deleted after 13 months.
  • Switching it off. Open Settings (the gear button) and tap "Statistics: On". The game stops sending events and deletes its installation number and any unsent events from your phone. We cannot link data already sent to you; it is deleted after 13 months.
  • Game updates. The game may download small content updates (art, sound, scenes) from our server capgo.jevgenia.com. The request contains only the game version; the server does not log IP addresses.
  • Google Play. The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect install and stability information under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Children. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The statistics above contain no personal information.

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions: hello@maumiuworks.com.
