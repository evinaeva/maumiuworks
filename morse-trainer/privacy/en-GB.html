Morsenta — Privacy Policy

Morsenta does not collect, share or sell any personal data. The only thing it sends is anonymous usage counts (see below), and you can turn that off.

  • Works offline. Everything works without an internet connection. The only thing the app sends is the anonymous usage statistics described below, and you can turn that off. It may also download updated texts and tables (see "Data updates"); without internet it uses the ones built into the app.
  • No account, no ads. There is no sign-in, no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics or crash reporting SDK and no advertising identifier.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. To learn which parts of the app are useful, the app counts on your phone, per day, which screens are opened, which screen was open when you left the app, which main buttons were pressed and which functions (for example, export) were used. Once a day it sends these counts to our own server (stats.jevgenia.com) together with the app version, the app language and the date. It sends no device or advertising identifier, no session identifier, no exact time, no location and nothing you entered. The server only adds the counts to daily totals per app version and does not keep the request itself. Like any internet request it sees your IP address; it uses it only in memory to deliver the request and to limit abuse, and never stores or logs it. The totals are kept for at most 25 months and are not shared with anyone. You can turn this off at any time in Settings ("Send anonymous usage statistics"); counts not yet sent are then deleted. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); the data is anonymous.
  • Data updates. The app may download updated texts and reference tables from our server (capgo.jevgenia.com). The request contains no identifiers about you or your device; like any internet request it reveals your IP address to the server, which uses it only to deliver the file and does not keep it.
  • Microphone (optional). The "Listen" feature in the translator decodes Morse tones from a radio or another phone. The app asks for microphone access only when you start it. The sound is analysed on your phone while you listen; it is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Everything else in the app works without this permission.
  • Camera (optional, beta). The "Light" feature in the translator decodes Morse from a blinking lamp or flashlight. The app asks for camera access only when you tap "Start the camera". Frames are analysed on your phone and dropped at once: nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Everything else in the app works without this permission.
  • Answer by voice (optional). In lessons you can say the answer instead of tapping it. On Android 12 and later the app first uses the phone's on-device speech recognition, and the sound does not leave the phone. If your phone cannot recognise the language offline, the app asks you before using the phone's online speech service (on most phones, Google's); only then the short phrase you say goes to that service to be recognised, under its provider's privacy policy. You can withdraw this in Settings. The app receives only the recognised text, uses it at once to check the answer and does not record, save or send the sound itself. Everything else in the app works without this.
  • Vibration and flashlight. Used only to play Morse code when you ask for it.
  • Your data stays on your phone. Your learning progress and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Backups are yours. If you save a backup, the file goes only where you choose (a folder on the phone or a cloud app you pick). The app does not read or upload it anywhere else.
  • Android system backup. If you have turned on Android's own backup, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup under your Google account. This is controlled by you in the phone's settings, not by the app.
  • Google Play. The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may ask you to rate the app or offer app updates through its own system screens; those are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions: hello@maumiuworks.com.
