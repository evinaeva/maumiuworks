Features

A tournament bracket on paper or in a spreadsheet falls apart as soon as one score changes. Ligona is a tournament bracket maker: it builds the knockout bracket, league table, groups with playoffs or Swiss rounds for you and updates everything after every score — in a minute, with no account and no limits on teams or tournaments. It has no ads and works without internet.

WHY ORGANIZERS SWITCH TO LIGONA

  • All 5 formats built in: single elimination, double elimination, league (round robin, once or home and away), groups + playoff, Swiss system.
  • No limits: 8, 16, 32, 64 or 100+ teams. Create as many tournaments as you like.
  • Works without internet: at the field, in the gym or anywhere with no signal.
  • No sign-up: open the app and start. Your data stays on the phone.

SET UP IN THREE STEPS

1. Pick the format.
2. Add teams or players: type them, paste a list from notes or a chat, or add "Team 1…16" in one tap. The order is the seeding; use Random draw to shuffle.
3. Name it, choose the sport and options — create.

ENTER A SCORE, THE REST IS AUTOMATIC

  • Big +/− buttons or type the score. Only the winner matters? One tap.
  • Level score in a knockout match? Choose who goes through (penalties, extra time, tie-break).
  • Winners move on, byes go to the top seeds automatically, tables update instantly.
  • "Up next" always shows the next match to play.
  • Fix any earlier result. If a different winner goes through, you are warned first and only the results that depended on it are cleared. Changed your mind? Undo.

BRACKETS THAT ARE EASY TO READ

  • Clear tournament bracket with rounds, seeds and connecting lines; pinch or use the +/− buttons to zoom.
  • Double elimination with winners and losers brackets and an optional grand final reset.
  • Third-place match, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final named for you.

LEAGUES, GROUPS AND SWISS

  • Fixture list by round and a points table: played, won, drawn, lost, difference, points.
  • Choose the points for a win, draw and loss, and how ties are broken: score difference first or head-to-head first. The rule is written under every table.
  • Groups + playoff: snake seeding into groups, choose how many advance; group winners avoid each other in the first knockout round.
  • Swiss system: players with equal scores meet, no rematches, fair byes, Buchholz tie-break, next round paired automatically.

MORE FOR ORGANIZERS

  • Quick match: a big scoreboard for one game, no tournament needed — tennis and padel scoring (15-30-40, games and sets) and pickleball included. Save the result into a tournament later.
  • Cricket: runs, wickets and overs, "No result" for rain, net run rate in the table.
  • Start from a template (5-a-side, office ping-pong, darts night and more) or try an example first.
  • Best-of-3, 5 or 7 series, set per round. No-show in one tap.
  • Date, time and venue for every match; saved lists of players; play again with the same teams.
  • Team colors and photos, or circles with initials.
  • A champion moment with a podium, ready to share as a picture.
  • Deleted tournaments wait 30 days in the trash.

SHARE WITH EVERYONE

Send the bracket or the table as a picture, the results as text, or a spreadsheet (CSV) to any messenger or e-mail.

YOUR RESULTS ARE SAFE

Every change is saved instantly on your phone, with a safety copy of the previous version. Save a backup file to move your tournaments to a new phone.

Questions and answers

How do I make a tournament bracket on my phone?

Open Ligona and pick a format: single elimination, double elimination, round robin, groups with playoffs or Swiss. Add teams or players by typing them, pasting a list from your notes or a chat, or adding numbered teams in one tap. The order is the seeding, and Random draw shuffles it. Name the tournament and create it. Then enter scores and share the bracket or table as a picture, the results as text, or a CSV spreadsheet.

Is there a round robin schedule generator, and how many games does it make?

Yes. Choose League (round robin) and Ligona builds the whole schedule round by round, so everyone plays everyone once, or twice with Home and away. A round robin of N teams has N(N-1)/2 games: 6 teams play 15, 8 teams play 28 and 10 teams play 45. With an even number of teams that takes N-1 rounds, with an odd number N rounds. Home and away doubles both numbers.

How are byes handled with an odd number of teams?

In a knockout bracket the field is filled up to the next power of two (4, 8, 16, 32…) and the byes go to the highest seeds automatically, so 12 teams get a 16-slot bracket with 4 byes. In a round robin one team sits out each round and gets no points for it. In the Swiss system the lowest-ranked player who has not had a bye yet gets one, and it counts as a win.

How does a double elimination bracket work?

A team is out only after its second loss. Everyone starts in the winners bracket; a first loss drops a team to the losers bracket, and a second loss ends its tournament. The two bracket champions meet in the grand final. If the losers bracket champion wins it, both have one loss, so Ligona can play a second final (Grand final reset, on or off). With N teams that is 2N-2 games, or 2N-1 when the reset is played.

How are ties in the standings broken?

By default, teams level on points are ranked by score difference, then points scored, then head-to-head results among the tied teams, then wins. If your league puts head-to-head first, choose that when you create the tournament. Swiss uses Buchholz, the total points of a player's opponents, then wins. You also set the points for a win, draw and loss. The rule is written under every table, and Why this place explains any position.

Does the tournament app work offline, and do I need an account?

No account and no internet are needed. Brackets, schedules, scores and tables all work offline, at the field or in a gym with no signal, and your tournaments stay on your phone. Save a backup file to move them to a new phone. There are no ads. The app sends anonymous daily usage counts, such as which screens were opened, and you can switch that off in Settings.
