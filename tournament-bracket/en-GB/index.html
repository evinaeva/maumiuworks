Features

A knockout draw on paper or in a spreadsheet falls apart as soon as one result changes. Ligona is a tournament maker: it builds the knockout cup, league table, groups with a knockout stage or Swiss rounds for you and updates the fixtures after every result — in a minute, with no account and no limits on teams or tournaments. It has no ads and works without internet.

WHY ORGANISERS SWITCH TO LIGONA

  • All 5 formats built in: single elimination, double elimination, league (round robin, once or home and away), groups + playoff, Swiss system.
  • No limits: 8, 16, 32, 64 or 100+ teams. Create as many tournaments as you like.
  • Works without internet: at the pitch, in the sports hall or anywhere with no signal.
  • No sign-up: open the app and start. Your data stays on the phone.

SET UP IN THREE STEPS

1. Pick the format.
2. Add teams or players: type them, paste a list from notes or a chat, or add "Team 1…16" in one tap. The order is the seeding; use Random draw to shuffle.
3. Name it, choose the sport and options — create.

ENTER A SCORE, THE REST IS AUTOMATIC

  • Big +/− buttons or type the score. Only the winner matters? One tap.
  • Level score in a knockout match? Choose who goes through (penalties, extra time, tie-break).
  • Winners move on, byes go to the top seeds automatically, tables update instantly.
  • "Up next" always shows the next match to play.
  • Fix any earlier result. If a different winner goes through, you are warned first and only the results that depended on it are cleared. Changed your mind? Undo.

BRACKETS THAT ARE EASY TO READ

  • Clear tournament bracket with rounds, seeds and connecting lines; pinch or use the +/− buttons to zoom.
  • Double elimination with winners and losers brackets and an optional grand final reset.
  • Third-place match, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final named for you.

LEAGUES, GROUPS AND SWISS

  • Fixture list by round and a points table: played, won, drawn, lost, difference, points.
  • Choose the points for a win, draw and loss, and how ties are broken: score difference first or head-to-head first. The rule is written under every table.
  • Groups + playoff: snake seeding into groups, choose how many advance; group winners avoid each other in the first knockout round.
  • Swiss system: players with equal scores meet, no rematches, fair byes, Buchholz tie-break, next round paired automatically.

MORE FOR ORGANISERS

  • Quick match: a big scoreboard for one game, no tournament needed — tennis and padel scoring (15-30-40, games and sets) and pickleball included. Save the result into a tournament later.
  • Cricket: runs, wickets and overs, "No result" for rain, net run rate in the table.
  • Start from a template (5-a-side, office ping-pong, darts night and more) or try an example first.
  • Best-of-3, 5 or 7 series, set per round. No-show in one tap.
  • Date, time and venue for every match; saved lists of players; play again with the same teams.
  • Team colours and photos, or circles with initials.
  • A champion moment with a podium, ready to share as a picture.
  • Deleted tournaments wait 30 days in the trash.

SHARE WITH EVERYONE

Send the bracket or the table as a picture, the results as text, or a spreadsheet (CSV) to any messenger or e-mail.

YOUR RESULTS ARE SAFE

Every change is saved instantly on your phone, with a safety copy of the previous version. Save a backup file to move your tournaments to a new phone.

Questions and answers

How do I create a league fixture list and table on my phone?

Open Ligona, choose League (round robin) and add the teams: type them, paste a list from your notes or a chat, or add numbered teams in one tap. Pick one round of fixtures or Home and away, set the points for a win, draw and loss, and create. Ligona draws up the fixtures round by round and updates the league table after every score: played, won, drawn, lost, difference and points.

How many fixtures are there in a round robin league?

A league where everyone plays everyone once has N(N-1)/2 fixtures for N teams: 6 teams play 15 matches, 8 teams 28, 10 teams 45 and 12 teams 66. With an even number of teams that takes N-1 rounds, with an odd number N rounds, because one team rests each round. Home and away doubles it, so 8 teams play 56 matches over 14 rounds. Ligona works all of this out for you.

What happens with an odd number of teams?

In a knockout the draw is filled up to the next 4, 8, 16 or 32 places and the byes go to the highest seeds automatically, so 12 teams get a 16-place draw with 4 byes. In a league one team sits out each round and gets no points for it. In the Swiss system the lowest-placed player without a bye so far gets one, and it counts as a win.

How are teams level on points separated in the league table?

By default the order is goal difference, then goals scored, then head-to-head results between the teams level on points, then wins. If your competition puts head-to-head first, switch that on when you set up the tournament. The rule is written under every table, and Why this place explains any position. Among teams level on points, one with fewer no-shows against the others ranks higher.

Can it work out net run rate for a cricket league?

Yes. Choose cricket as the sport and enter each result as runs and wickets, with overs if you count them. League points are 2 for a win and 1 for a tie or no result, and net run rate separates teams level on points once overs are entered for all of them. A side bowled out counts as having faced its full overs. T20 is the default, and you can set up to 50 overs.

Does the tournament maker work offline?

Yes. Fixtures, scores and tables all work without internet, at the pitch, in the sports hall or at the pub, and there is no account to create. Your tournaments stay on your phone; save a backup file to move them to a new one. There are no ads. The app sends anonymous daily usage counts, such as which screens were opened, and you can turn that off in Settings.
