Features

Lost track of which goat got what dose, and when it can be sold? Pecuo keeps every animal's record on your phone: ear tag, weights, births, vaccinations and withdrawal dates. Made for beef and dairy herds, goat and sheep farms, homesteads and 4-H livestock records.

No ads. It works without internet and without signing in, with no limit on animals; cloud copies only if you want them.

ANIMALS

  • Ear tag, official ID, name, photo, species (cattle, goats, sheep, buffalo, camels or your own), breed and birth date
  • Life stage shown automatically: kid, doeling, heifer, ewe lamb, bull…
  • Mother and father from your herd or from another farm, offspring, 3-generation pedigree
  • Groups and pens, origin (born here, purchased), tag history when you re-tag
  • Search by tag, name or old tag; filter by species, group, sex and status; sort by weight

EVENTS AND HERD BOOK

  • Weighing with gain and grams per day, milk records, breeding, pregnancy with an expected birth date you can adjust
  • Kidding, lambing and calving: each newborn gets its own card linked to its parents
  • Vaccinations, deworming and treatments with dose, next dose and separate meat and milk withdrawal periods
  • Weaning, dry-off, hoof trimming, sales, purchases, deaths, income and expenses
  • Group events: select several animals and record a vaccination once, or weigh the whole group in one list
  • Plan tasks for later and tick them off when done

TAG SCANNER

  • Point the camera at the ear tag: it reads the barcode or QR code on official tags and the number printed on visual tags
  • Finds the animal right away, or adds a new one with that tag number
  • Also reads a photo of the tag; works on the phone, no internet needed
  • Bluetooth stick readers for electronic (EID) tags that work as a keyboard: the number goes into the search and the animal opens

MONEY

  • A Money tab for farm income and expenses: feed, vet and medicine, labor, milk sold, animal sales, subsidies and more
  • Profit or loss by month and by category
  • Link an entry to one animal or a group; animal sales and purchases count too
  • Export to Excel

REMINDERS

  • Coming up: expected births, next doses, planned tasks and the end of withdrawal periods
  • One quiet daily reminder that you can switch off

REPORTS AND EXPORT

  • Herd count by species, sex and group; births, deaths, sales, milk, income and expenses by period
  • Export to Excel (xlsx) and CSV, printable PDF card with pedigree
  • Import your herd from a spreadsheet to get started in minutes

CALCULATORS

  • Gestation calculator for goats, sheep, cattle, buffalo and camels
  • Withdrawal period calculator for meat and milk
  • Feed and hay calculator

EASY START

  • A short guided start: language, your animals, units and text size
  • Try the app with a sample herd and remove it with one tap
  • Or import your herd from a spreadsheet, or restore a backup

EASY TO USE

  • Text size and large buttons, easy to tap with gloves
  • Color-blind friendly and high-contrast colors, dark theme
  • Clear bottom menu: Herd, Coming up, Money, Reports, More

YOUR DATA STAYS SAFE

  • Backup with photos to a file, plus automatic backup to a folder such as Google Drive
  • Copies to your own Google Drive or Dropbox if you want: automatic or with one tap, and you can disconnect at any time
  • Shared farm if you want: family and workers on their own phones, with roles — owner, family, worker (no money, no deleting), view only
  • Restore on a new phone in a few taps
  • Records are kept on your phone; only anonymous usage counts are sent, and you can turn them off

Units in kg, lb or arroba, liters or gallons, and your currency.
