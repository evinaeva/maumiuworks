Features

Lost track of which goat got what dose, and when it can be sold? Pecuo keeps every animal's record on your phone: ear tag, weights, births, vaccinations and withdrawal dates. Made for beef and dairy herds, goat and sheep farms, homesteads and 4-H livestock records.

No ads. It works without internet and without signing in, with no limit on animals; cloud copies only if you want them.

ANIMALS

  • Ear tag, official ID, name, photo, species (cattle, goats, sheep, buffalo, camels or your own), breed and birth date
  • Life stage shown automatically: kid, doeling, heifer, ewe lamb, bull…
  • Mother and father from your herd or from another farm, offspring, 3-generation pedigree
  • Groups and pens, origin (born here, purchased), tag history when you re-tag
  • Search by tag, name or old tag; filter by species, group, sex and status; sort by weight

EVENTS AND HERD BOOK

  • Weighing with gain and grams per day, milk records, breeding, pregnancy with an expected birth date you can adjust
  • Kidding, lambing and calving: each newborn gets its own card linked to its parents
  • Vaccinations, deworming and treatments with dose, next dose and separate meat and milk withdrawal periods
  • Weaning, dry-off, hoof trimming, sales, purchases, deaths, income and expenses
  • Group events: select several animals and record a vaccination once, or weigh the whole group in one list
  • Plan tasks for later and tick them off when done

TAG SCANNER

  • Point the camera at the ear tag: it reads the barcode or QR code on official tags and the number printed on visual tags
  • Finds the animal right away, or adds a new one with that tag number
  • Also reads a photo of the tag; works on the phone, no internet needed
  • Bluetooth stick readers for electronic (EID) tags that work as a keyboard: the number goes into the search and the animal opens

MONEY

  • A Money tab for farm income and expenses: feed, vet and medicine, labor, milk sold, animal sales, subsidies and more
  • Profit or loss by month and by category
  • Link an entry to one animal or a group; animal sales and purchases count too
  • Export to Excel

REMINDERS

  • Coming up: expected births, next doses, planned tasks and the end of withdrawal periods
  • One quiet daily reminder that you can switch off

REPORTS AND EXPORT

  • Herd count by species, sex and group; births, deaths, sales, milk, income and expenses by period
  • Export to Excel (xlsx) and CSV, printable PDF card with pedigree
  • Import your herd from a spreadsheet to get started in minutes

CALCULATORS

  • Gestation calculator for goats, sheep, cattle, buffalo and camels
  • Withdrawal period calculator for meat and milk
  • Feed and hay calculator

EASY START

  • A short guided start: language, your animals, units and text size
  • Try the app with a sample herd and remove it with one tap
  • Or import your herd from a spreadsheet, or restore a backup

EASY TO USE

  • Text size and large buttons, easy to tap with gloves
  • Color-blind friendly and high-contrast colors, dark theme
  • Clear bottom menu: Herd, Coming up, Money, Reports, More

YOUR DATA STAYS SAFE

  • Backup with photos to a file, plus automatic backup to a folder such as Google Drive
  • Copies to your own Google Drive or Dropbox if you want: automatic or with one tap, and you can disconnect at any time
  • Shared farm if you want: family and workers on their own phones, with roles — owner, family, worker (no money, no deleting), view only
  • Restore on a new phone in a few taps
  • Records are kept on your phone; only anonymous usage counts are sent, and you can turn them off

Units in kg, lb or arroba, liters or gallons, and your currency.

Questions and answers

How do I keep cattle records on my phone?

Install Pecuo and tap Add animal. Each cow, calf or bull gets its own card with ear tag, name, breed, birth date, photo, mother and father, and you add weights, treatments, breeding and calving to it over time. To find an animal later, search by tag number or point the camera at the ear tag. To start faster, save your herd list from Excel as CSV and import it, or try a sample herd first. There is no limit on the number of animals.

Is there a livestock app that works offline, without internet?

Yes. Pecuo works fully without internet and without an account: animal cards, reminders, reports, calculators and the ear tag scanner all run on the phone, even in a barn or pasture with no signal. The online features are optional: cloud copies and the shared farm, which sync when you are back in coverage. When there is a connection, the app also sends anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in Settings, and may download updated texts and reference tables.

How do I track vaccinations, treatments and withdrawal periods?

Open the animal and add a Vaccination, Deworming or Treatment record with the product, dose and next dose. Enter the meat and milk withdrawal days from the medicine label or your vet's instructions, and Pecuo shows the date until which that animal's milk or meat should not be sold. Next doses and the end of each withdrawal period appear under Coming up and in one daily reminder. To treat a whole group, long-press to select several animals and record it once.

How do I record calving and keep track of due dates?

When a pregnancy is confirmed, add a Pregnancy record: Pecuo suggests the expected birth date from the usual gestation of the species, and you can change it. Expected births show under Coming up and in the daily reminder. When the cow calves, add a Birth record on her card and type a tag for each calf: every newborn gets its own card already linked to its mother, ready for weights, weaning and vaccinations. Kidding and lambing are recorded the same way.

Can I keep goat and sheep records too?

Yes. Pecuo is made for mixed farms: beef and dairy cattle, goats, sheep, buffalo, camels or a species you add yourself. Life stages such as kid, doeling, ewe lamb or heifer are shown automatically. Twins and triplets each get their own card. Milk records, dry-off, hoof trimming, deworming and weights work for every species, and the gestation calculator covers goats and sheep as well. It also suits homesteads and 4-H livestock records.

Can my family and farm workers share the same herd records?

Yes, if you want to. In Settings, Shared farm, the owner signs in with Google, starts a farm from the herd on the phone and sends an invite code to each person; a code works once within 7 days. Everyone sees the same herd on their own phone with a role: Owner, Family, Worker (adds and edits records, but does not see money and cannot delete) or View only. Without signal the app keeps working and syncs later.

How do I back up my herd to Google Drive or move it to a new phone?

In Settings, Where to keep copies, tap Sign in with Google: copies with photos go to a hidden app folder on your own Google Drive, manually, after changes, once a day or once a week. Dropbox, a folder of your choice or a backup file work too. On the new phone, install Pecuo, sign in with the same Google account and restore the copy. We never receive your copies, and your records always stay on the phone as well.

How to keep livestock health records
