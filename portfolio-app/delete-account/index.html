Delete your Pecuo account and data

Pecuo (by Jevgenia) needs an account only for the optional shared farm. You create it when you sign in with Google in Settings → "Shared farm". This page explains how to delete that account and the data stored with it.

What is deleted

  • Your Pecuo account in Firebase Authentication (your account ID, Google name and e-mail address).
  • Your name, e-mail address and role in the farm's member list.
  • If you are the farm's Owner: the whole farm in the cloud — its name, all its herd records (animals, events, groups, money records) and its members.

What stays

  • The herd on your phone always stays: animals, records, photos and settings are not touched. To remove them too, uninstall the app or clear its data in the phone's settings.
  • If you are not the owner, the records you added to someone else's farm stay in that farm, because they are part of its herd. The copies already synced to other members' phones stay on those phones.
  • Anonymous usage counts are not linked to any account and cannot be deleted per person.

Delete in the app 1. Open Pecuo → Settings → "Shared farm". 2. Tap "Delete my account" and confirm. 3. If the app asks, sign in again with Google and repeat.

You can also only leave a farm ("Leave the farm") or, as the owner, delete just the farm ("Delete the farm from the cloud") and keep your account.

Delete without the app Send an e-mail to hello@maumiuworks.com from the e-mail address of your Google account, with the subject "Delete my Pecuo account". We delete the account and the data listed above within 30 days and reply when it is done.
