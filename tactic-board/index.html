Features

Explaining a play with words and a pen never sticks. Tacticana is a soccer tactics board and lineup builder for coaches: set your soccer lineup and formation, draw plays with arrows and animate them step by step, then send the video to the team. No ads, no sign-up, no account and no internet needed: it works offline and your boards stay on your phone.

A FIELD FOR EVERY FORMAT

  • 11v11, 9v9, 8v8, 7v7, 6v6, 5v5, 4v4, 3v3, futsal and half field
  • Horizontal or vertical layout, several field styles
  • Made for tablets and landscape too

PLAYERS AND EQUIPMENT

  • Two teams with jersey numbers and names, goalkeepers in their own color
  • Ball, cones, flat markers, poles, agility ladder and mini goals for drills
  • Drag everything with your finger

DRAW THE PLAY

  • Arrows for passes (dashed), runs and dribbling (wavy), straight or curved
  • Lines, zones and text notes
  • Freehand drawing for anything else
  • Undo and redo

PLAYS THAT MOVE

  • Draw your arrows and tap “+ Step”: the players and the ball move along them
  • A pass sends the ball to the receiver’s feet, a run moves the player, a dribble takes the player forward with the ball
  • Movement follows curved arrows, so a curved run looks like one
  • Play it back as an animation and set the speed, a clear way to show set pieces and movement off the ball
  • Ready-made plays to start from: build-up from the back, overlap on the wing, corner kick and a 4v2 rondo

FORMATIONS AND LINEUP

  • Formation maker with ready-made formations: 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 3-5-2, 3-4-3, 5-3-2 and more
  • Templates for 7v7, 5v5 and futsal
  • A lineup list on every board: type numbers and names in seconds, names show under the players and move with them
  • Squad builder: save a team’s numbers and names as a squad (for example U12) and fill any board with it: players are matched by shirt number, the rest in order

SAVE AND SHARE

  • Unlimited boards, folders and search
  • Duplicate a board to try a variation
  • Export the whole play as an MP4 video or an animated GIF, or a step as a PNG image, and send it to your team chat
  • Save the video or image straight to your phone’s gallery
  • Print or send a PDF: one step per page, with your note for the players
  • Send the board file to another coach to open in their app
  • Back up all boards and squads to one file and restore them on a new phone

Your boards stay on your device; anonymous stats can be turned off in settings. Light and dark theme. Accessibility: color-blind mode, high contrast, large buttons and adjustable text size.

Made for youth and club coaches, rec and travel teams, PE teachers, players and anyone who loves talking soccer tactics.

Questions and answers

How do I make a soccer lineup and formation on my phone?

Create a board, pick the field size and tap a formation such as 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1 or 3-5-2, and the players line up in position. Open the lineup list and type jersey numbers and names: they show under the players and move with them. Save the numbers and names as a squad, for example U12, and fill any new board with it in seconds.

Is there an app to animate soccer plays step by step?

Yes. In Tacticana you draw arrows for passes, runs and dribbles, then tap "+ Step": the players and the ball move along the arrows, curved ones included. A pass sends the ball to the receiver's feet, and a dribble takes the player forward with the ball. Press Play to watch the whole play and change the speed. Ready-made plays like a corner kick and a 4v2 rondo show how it works.

Can I use it for 7v7, 9v9 or futsal?

Yes. Each board can be 11v11, 9v9, 8v8, 7v7, 6v6, 5v5, 4v4 or 3v3, a futsal court or half a field. Formations are ready for every format, and small-sided games can be set up with or without a goalkeeper. Show the field horizontally or vertically and pick the field style you like. It works on tablets and in landscape too.

How do I export a soccer play as a video?

Open the board and tap Share. Export the whole play as an MP4 video or an animated GIF, or a single step as a PNG image, and send it through the share menu to your team chat, email or any other app. On Android 10 and later you can also save the video or image to your phone's gallery. The file is created on the phone and needs no internet.

How can I share the lineup with my players and their parents?

Export the board as a PNG image with jersey numbers and names on the field and post it in the team group chat. For a printout, make a PDF with one step per page and your note for the players. If another coach uses Tacticana, send them the board file so they can open and edit the same board.

Does the soccer tactics app work offline, without an account?

Yes. Every feature works without an internet connection, and there is no sign-up, no account and no ads. Your boards, folders and squads stay on your phone, and you can back them all up to one file and restore them on a new phone. The app counts anonymous usage statistics, such as which screens are opened, and you can turn that off in Settings.
