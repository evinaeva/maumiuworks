Relaxing cleaning games are the ones with no timer, no lives, no energy meter and no way to fail: you scrub, rinse and polish at your own pace, and the reward is watching the dirt disappear. On Android, good examples are Oddly Satisfying Restoration (our game), Satisfying Cleaning Deep Clean and Unpacking, which all say they have no timers; Assemble With Care and ReStory add a story to repairing things, and A Little to the Left turns tidying into gentle puzzles. Before you install, read the listing for the words "timer", "lives" and "energy", and try the first session in airplane mode if you want to play offline.

What makes cleaning games so satisfying?

Cleaning games compress a real chore into its most rewarding part: the moment a dull, dirty thing becomes clean. A few design ingredients do most of the work:

  • Visible progress under your finger. Each swipe removes a layer you can see, so effort and result are directly linked. You always know how far you have come.
  • A clear before and after. The item starts grimy and ends bright, often with a final shine, which gives a small, complete sense of closure.
  • Touch, sound and vibration together. Brushing, foam and running water, with a short buzz on each stroke, make the action feel physical. Headphones make the sound side even stronger.
  • Small, finite tasks. One object, one room or one box at a time. You can stop after one item and still feel you finished something.
  • Order from mess. Sorting and tidying games use the same pull: putting things where they belong.

Sound matters more than it seems. In a 2018 laboratory study in PLOS ONE, people who experience ASMR (the tingling, calming response some people get to soft, repetitive sounds) saw their heart rate fall by about 3.4 beats per minute on average while watching ASMR videos, a larger drop than in people who do not experience ASMR. That study was about videos, not games, but it explains why soft cleaning sounds are a core feature of the genre.

What counts as "no pressure" in a cleaning game?

Many cleaning games look calm in their screenshots but add pressure through their systems. These are the usual sources:

Pressure sourceWhat it looks likeWhy it matters
TimerA countdown, a clock or a star rating for speedTurns relaxing into racing
Lives or fail stateMistakes cost a life, or a level can be lostYou play nervously instead of relaxing
EnergyEach level costs energy that refills over timeYou cannot play when you want to
Daily streak penaltyMissing a day resets progressCreates obligation

A truly calm game has none of these, or makes them optional. Unpacking, for example, says on its listing that it has no timers, meters or scores. A Little to the Left lets you skip a puzzle with its "Let It Be" option.

Which relaxing cleaning and restoration games are on Android?

All of these had a live Google Play listing when we checked on November 26, 2026. Prices and features can change, so read the listing before you buy.

Cleaning and restoring objects

  • Oddly Satisfying Restoration (Maumiu Works, our game): you inherit your grandpa's neglected shop and clean old things by hand, brushing off cobwebs, chipping away dried mud, spraying foam, scrubbing, rinsing and drying. No timers, no lives, no energy and no game over. Details below.
  • Satisfying Cleaning Deep Clean (Laplace Games): restore messy houses room by room, including furniture repair and carpet cleaning; the listing says it has no timers and plays offline.
  • ASMR Cleaning: Restore & Relax (Laplace Games): before-and-after transformations of floors, rooms and worn furniture with simple touch controls.
  • ASMR Hotel Clean: Power Wash (Lion Roar): power wash and deep clean resort rooms, patios and pools in 3D.
  • Home Restoration - House Decor (Panteon): clean, pressure wash and renovate an old house, then decorate it.

Repairing things, with a story

  • Assemble With Care (ustwo games): you play an antique restorer who repairs old-school objects for the people of the sun-soaked town of Bellariva; each puzzle is about working out how to take an object apart and restore it.
  • ReStory: Chill Repair Shop (tinyBuild): take apart, clean and repair Y2K-era gadgets in an electronics repair shop inspired by mid-2000s urban Japan, with a branching story. You also manage the shop's money, so it has more management than pure cleaning.

Tidying and unpacking

  • Unpacking (Balor Games): a BAFTA-winning game about taking things out of boxes and fitting them into a new home over eight house moves; paid up front, with no timers, meters or scores.
  • A Little to the Left (Secret Mode): sort, stack and arrange household objects, with a mischievous cat; you can try some puzzles first, and a one-time in-app purchase opens the full game.

Note that PowerWash Simulator is not on Android: its developer, FuturLab, says in its support FAQ that it has no current plans to bring the mobile version to Android, and the mobile game is on Apple Arcade. Google Play has several unrelated "power wash" games with similar names.

How can I check a cleaning game before installing it?

  1. Read the full listing, not just the screenshots. Look for clear statements like "no timers" or "no energy". If the listing talks about "boosters", "hearts" or "lives", expect pressure.
  2. Check the "Contains ads" and "In-app purchases" lines under the title on Google Play, so you know what to expect.
  3. Test offline. Turn on airplane mode before your first real session. A game that plays offline will simply start; others will ask for a connection.
  4. Play the first ten minutes with sound on and off. If the satisfaction disappears with sound off, use headphones; if the game never shows a timer or fail screen in that time, it is probably calm all the way.
  5. Look at the length. Story games like Unpacking or Assemble With Care end; level-based games keep adding levels. Both are fine, but know which one you want.

What is Oddly Satisfying Restoration like?

Oddly Satisfying Restoration is a calm cleaning and restoration game for Android phones. You inherit your grandpa's neglected shop in a quiet little town. The door is boarded up and the sign has faded, and your animal neighbors bring belongings they are not ready to throw away. You clean them by hand, with soft cleaning sounds and gentle vibration on every swipe, and learn the stories behind them. Along the way you beat the dust out of an old rug, work shampoo into it, stitch a torn seam, dye faded fabric and polish a dull trophy until it shines.

There are no timers, no lives, no energy and no game over, so you cannot fail; each item takes as long as you want. The whole game plays offline and needs no account. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, progress is saved there too. Sound and vibration switch on and off separately in Settings, and a short hint with a finger gesture shows what to do with each tool, so you can also play silently.

It is honest to say the game is short for now: the current version has one story chapter. You open the boarded-up door, tidy the shop, restore a toy mouse, name and paint your sign, then help Tilda the turtle with her rug, her boots and an old trophy. The For fun shelf holds eight items to clean again any time.

Questions and answers

Are there cleaning games with no timer?

Yes. Oddly Satisfying Restoration has no timers, lives, energy or game over, and Unpacking says on its listing that it has no timers, meters or scores. Satisfying Cleaning Deep Clean also states on its listing that it has no timers.

Can I play relaxing cleaning games offline?

Many of them, yes. Oddly Satisfying Restoration plays fully offline with no account, and Satisfying Cleaning Deep Clean lists offline play. For any other game, start it once in airplane mode to check.

Why are cleaning games relaxing?

They give visible progress for every small action, a clear before and after, and soft sounds and vibration that make each stroke feel physical. Without timers or fail states, there is nothing to worry about except the next spot of dirt.

Is PowerWash Simulator on Android?

No. The developer, FuturLab, says it has no current plans to bring the mobile version to Android; it is available on Apple Arcade. Similar-sounding games on Google Play are made by other developers.

Can I play Oddly Satisfying Restoration with the sound off?

Yes. Open Settings with the gear button and switch Sound and Vibration off separately. The dirt fades under your finger and a hint with a finger gesture shows what each tool does, so sound is not needed to play.
