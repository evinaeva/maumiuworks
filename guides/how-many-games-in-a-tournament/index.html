How many games are in a tournament?

The number of games depends on the format. With N teams, single elimination has N-1 games, double elimination has 2N-2 (2N-1 if the grand final is reset), a round robin has N(N-1)/2 (twice that with home and away), and a Swiss tournament has rounds × N/2 games, rounded down for an odd N. For 16 teams that is 15, 30 or 31, 120 and 32 games (Swiss in 4 rounds).

Why does single elimination have N-1 games?

Every game eliminates exactly one team, and all teams except the champion must be eliminated. So 16 teams need 15 games, 100 teams need 99. Byes do not change this: with 12 teams in a 16-slot bracket, the 4 byes are not games, and the total is still 11.

The number of rounds is log2 N rounded up: 4 rounds for 9 to 16 teams, 5 rounds for 17 to 32.

Why does double elimination have 2N-2 or 2N-1 games?

Every game gives exactly one team a loss. All teams except the champion are eliminated after two losses, which is 2(N-1) = 2N-2 games. If the losers bracket champion beats the unbeaten winners bracket champion in the grand final, both have one loss and a reset final is played: 2N-1 games. See double elimination explained for a full 8-team walkthrough.

How many games are in a round robin?

Each pair of teams meets once, and there are N(N-1)/2 pairs. Each team plays N-1 games. The schedule needs N-1 rounds for an even N and N rounds for an odd N (one team rests each round). Home and away (a double round robin) doubles both: 10 teams play 90 games over 18 rounds, 18 games per team. This is the format of most national football leagues.

How many games are in groups followed by playoffs?

Add the group games and the knockout games:

  • Group games = number of groups × s(s-1)/2, where s is the group size.
  • Knockout games = number of teams that advance - 1.
  • Add 1 for a third-place match.

How many games are in a Swiss tournament?

In each round every player is paired, so a round has N/2 games (rounded down for an odd N, where one player gets a bye). Total games = rounds × N/2. The usual number of rounds is at least log2 N rounded up, which is enough to separate a single player with a perfect score from the rest: 5 rounds for 32 players, 7 for 128. See Swiss system explained for how pairing works.

How many games does each team play?

The total is only half the story; players care about their own number of games.

For 8 teams: single elimination gives 1 to 3 games per team, double elimination 2 to 7, round robin 7 each, Swiss 3 each.

How long will the tournament take with several courts?

Games can only be played as fast as courts become available and as the rounds allow. Work out the number of time slots, then multiply by the length of one slot (game plus changeover):

  • For each round, slots = games in that round ÷ courts, rounded up.
  • Add up the slots of all rounds.

Example 1: 16-team single elimination on 4 courts with 30-minute slots. Round 1 has 8 games (2 slots), the quarterfinals 4 (1 slot), the semifinals 2 (1 slot) and the final 1 (1 slot): 5 slots, 2 hours 30 minutes.

Example 2: 8-team round robin on 2 courts with 20-minute slots. 7 rounds of 4 games, 2 slots per round: 14 slots, 4 hours 40 minutes. On 4 courts it is 7 slots, 2 hours 20 minutes.

Example 3: 8-team double elimination on 2 courts. Winners round 1 takes 2 slots; winners semifinals and losers round 1 share the next 2 slots; then losers round 2 (1 slot), winners final with losers round 3 (1 slot), losers final (1 slot), grand final (1 slot): 8 slots, 9 if the final is reset. That assumes you overlap rounds of the two brackets; played strictly one round after another it is 9 slots, or 10 with the reset, which is what the calculator shows.

Notice that the late rounds of any knockout use only one or two courts, so adding courts mostly speeds up the early rounds. A round robin uses all courts every round, so it scales almost perfectly with the number of courts. Also plan for rest: a team should not play two slots back to back if the games are physically hard.

Which format should you choose for the time you have?

  • Little time, many teams: single elimination (N-1 games), but half the field plays only once.
  • A bit more time, and everyone should play at least twice: double elimination (about 2N games) or groups + playoffs.
  • A small group and everyone should play everyone: round robin, ideally 4 to 10 teams.
  • Many players and a fixed number of rounds: Swiss, where everyone plays every round.

Questions and answers

How many games are in a 16-team single elimination tournament?

15 games over 4 rounds: 8, 4, 2 and 1. Add 1 if you play a third-place match.

How many games are in a round robin with 10 teams?

45 games: 10 × 9 ÷ 2. Each team plays 9 games over 9 rounds. With home and away it is 90 games.

How many games are in a 32-team double elimination bracket?

62 games, or 63 if the grand final is reset: 2 × 32 - 2 = 62.

Do byes count as games?

No. A bye is an empty slot, so it does not change any of the formulas. A 12-team single elimination bracket has 11 games even though it is drawn on 16 lines.

How many games are in a 12-team tournament?

It depends on the format: 11 in single elimination, 22 or 23 in double elimination, 66 in a round robin, 19 in four groups of 3 with an 8-team playoff, and 24 in a 4-round Swiss.
